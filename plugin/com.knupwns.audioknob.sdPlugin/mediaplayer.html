The key follows whatever Windows is playing - Spotify, a browser tab, VLC - and pressing it plays or pauses. The glyph shows what the press would do: a pause bar while it plays, a play triangle while it is stopped.

A line too long for the key scrolls left and right rather than shrinking to fit. The glyph takes whatever room the lines leave, so it grows when you hide one and shrinks when both sit on the same side.
